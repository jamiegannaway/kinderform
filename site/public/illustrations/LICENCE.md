# Illustrations: not openly licensed

The drawings in this folder come from **Notioly**, a paid illustration pack. Kinderform bought a licence to use them on its websites.

They are **not** covered by this repository's MIT licence (code) or CC BY 4.0 licence (writing and data). Do not copy, reuse or redistribute these files. To use Notioly illustrations in your own work, buy your own licence from Notioly.

Only the drawings that kinderform.org shows are kept here, in the form the site serves them: shrunk with SVGO, with colours set as attributes so the site's Content-Security-Policy does not block them, and the ink set to the site's foreground colour.
