# Get help with Kinderform

Kinderform helps fostering and adoption services improve their first reply. If you want to foster or adopt, contact your local service directly. [Find a service](https://www.kinderform.org/#find-a-service) links to UK guidance. For other countries, [browse listed service pages](https://www.kinderform.org/compare/forms) and check the details on the service’s own website. The [example forms](https://www.kinderform.org/example-form) are demonstrations. They send nothing to a service.

## What would you like to do?

| I want to… | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| Build a new form or adapt one in our own system | [Choose a starter for any builder](https://www.kinderform.org/build). Use the shared version or pick the wording for your location. |
| Review the form my service uses | Try an [example form](https://www.kinderform.org/example-form). Compare your questions and the reply time you promise with the [draft checklist](rubric/rubric.md). |
| Understand how to record a first reply | Follow [one made-up enquiry](docs/one-enquiry.md) from the form to a conversation. |
| Correct something about a service | [Report a correction](https://github.com/jamiegannaway/kinderform/issues/new?template=correction.yml) or email Jamie. Give the public page, the change and the date you checked. |
| Suggest evidence, ask about the tools or report a fault | [Open a project question or suggestion](https://github.com/jamiegannaway/kinderform/issues/new?template=project-help.yml) or email Jamie. For research, include the original source and what it adds. |
| Share what the first reply felt like | Email Jamie privately. Describe what helped or could improve. Leave out names and details that might identify anyone. |
| Reply on behalf of a service about its draft findings | Email Jamie privately with the page or record you mean. Each service can review its own findings before Kinderform publishes a named score. |

You can contact Jamie without a GitHub account. Email [jamie.gannaway@gmail.com](mailto:jamie.gannaway@gmail.com).

## Report a privacy or security problem privately

If you find personal data, a named worker or a security problem, email [jamie@kinderform.org](mailto:jamie@kinderform.org) and follow the [private reporting guide](SECURITY.md). Give the page or file location. Leave out the personal details, screenshots and case records. GitHub issues are public. To see, correct or delete what Kinderform holds about you, read the [privacy policy](https://www.kinderform.org/privacy).

To offer a change to the files, see [how to contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md).
