# Code of conduct

This project touches the lives of children, carers and the people who work with them. We hold ourselves to a high standard of care in how we talk and work.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Respect lived experience. Carers and care-experienced people are experts here.
- Criticise systems and ideas, not people.
- Protect privacy. Never share identifying details of children, carers, applicants or staff.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Sharing personal data or confidential case information.
- Naming or shaming individual workers.
- Using this project to sell products or services.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's contact options. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content or contributors that break this code.

This code draws on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
