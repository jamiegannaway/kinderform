# Tribal front doors: search notes (2026-11-14)

All rows are `search_result` / `unverified`. Pages were not opened.

## Tribes with approved Title IV-E plans

Source: Children's Bureau, Tribes with Approved Title IV-E Plans,
https://acf.gov/cb/grant-funding/tribes-approved-title-iv-e-plans (also listed on data.gov:
https://catalog-old.data.gov/dataset/tribes-with-approved-title-iv-e-plans). The page itself could not be opened.
Search metadata suggests the ACF page may be old. A 2025 secondary source
(https://www.gksnetwork.org/resources/family-first-prevention-services-and-programs/) says 22 tribal nations
have an approved plan and 13 have implemented it. The full list of 22 was not visible in search snippets.

Names confirmed in snippets as on the ACF list (all have a row):
- Navajo Nation (AZ; also NM, UT)
- Salt River Pima-Maricopa Indian Community (AZ)
- Pascua Yaqui Tribe (AZ)
- Tolowa Dee-ni' Nation (CA)
- Port Gamble S'Klallam Tribe (WA)
- South Puget Intertribal Planning Agency (WA, consortium)
- Confederated Salish and Kootenai Tribes (MT)
- Keweenaw Bay Indian Community (MI)
- Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians (NC)
- Cherokee Nation (OK)

Not confirmed, recorded as `selection: search`: Prairie Band Potawatomi Nation (KS), Sault Ste. Marie Tribe of
Chippewa Indians (MI; has a IV-E agreement with Michigan). Roughly 10 further approved tribes remain unidentified.
