# Evidence digest: November 2026

## What was added

**A new review: [intake interactions](../reports/2026-11-intake-interactions.md).** It covers everything that happens after someone gets in touch about fostering or adoption, up to a formal application: acknowledgements, first calls, follow-ups, information events, home visits, contact with experienced carers and adopters, "not now" handling, hand-offs between organisations, digital and AI tools, and withdrawal conversations. It was produced with an AI-assisted research tool. A few citations were checked by hand on 14 November 2026. The rest have not yet been checked against the originals.

**Library entries.** Seven entries were added in a new section 4, "What happens after someone enquires". Three more were added to section 2: Rodger et al. 2006, Randle et al. 2014 and Sebba 2012. Four official sources were added to the policy and statistics list. The "Gaps we know about" list was rewritten.

## Why it matters

1. **Speed alone is not enough.** The controlled evaluation of Foster with North East found initial visits got much faster, but there was no measurable change in applications or approvals compared with similar areas. The project's case for fast replies still stands, because people who are never reached are the biggest early loss. But we should not claim that speed on its own leads to more approvals.
2. **Continuity and hand-offs are where people are lost now.** The hand-off from a hub to a council is the most clearly documented weak point. DfE now requires a named "journey guide" in end-to-end hubs.
3. **Named, personal messages are the most transferable behavioural finding.** The evidence comes from a jobcentre trial, not from fostering, so it needs testing here.
4. **Correction.** The often-quoted 61% withdrawal figure is 61% of *completed* applications (3,290 of 5,370 in 2024–25), not of all applications received. This was checked against Ofsted's main findings.
5. **Proposals for the standard.** The review proposes a refined first-contact standard and a new `Interaction` record, with one row per touchpoint, to sit alongside the Enquiry record. These are for discussion only. Nothing in `schema/` has changed.
